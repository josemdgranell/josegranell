PERSONAL CONCEPT / AI workspace · Web application

Context

Think with evidence.

An AI workspace for turning scattered research into decisions you can explain. Bring the context, explore the alternatives and stay in control.

THE DESIGN PROCESS

Useful AI starts
with better questions.

A polished answer can hide weak assumptions. Context is a self-initiated web concept for product teams: bring the evidence together, explore different directions and keep the final decision with the person doing the work.

01

Design the context, not just the chat

I started with a focused task: make a first-workout experience easier to understand. Selectable source cards make the input visible, while source references connect each proposal back to its supporting information.

02

Make alternatives and uncertainty visible

Instead of one authoritative answer, the interface compares two directions, their evidence and their trade-offs. Missing context stays visible. A draft only becomes a decision after explicit review, with an undo option.

03

Explore with AI. Resolve through design.

The concept was developed through an AI-assisted workflow: defining the brief, generating interface explorations, refining the visual system and building a responsive HTML, CSS and JavaScript prototype. The working interaction tests source selection, comparison and reversible decisions.

A concept you can try

Personal exploration · Product design, AI interaction patterns and front-end prototyping. The example interviews, outputs and decision history are fictional. The prototype runs locally in the browser; a production version would need retrieval, model integration, evaluation and access controls.
